      About

      KR

      Karthik Ragubathy

      Hi there, I am Karthik Ragubathy and I build fun ideas like these to scratch an itch. I made this site based on Tim Urban's Your Life in Weeks on Wait But Why, where the idea of one square per week comes from. Everything here is saved only in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. If you liked it or have feedback or questions, please reach out to me at me[at]pkarthik.in.

      Life expectancy figures are the World Health Organization’s estimates of life expectancy at birth (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO), saved with this site so nothing is looked up when you use it.
